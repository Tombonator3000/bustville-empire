# Early Game Talent Pool and Shabby Start

Purpose: make early Bustville feel scrappy, poor, local, and rewarding while preserving consent-only adult business framing.

## Phase philosophy
- Trailer Park (Lv 1-2): rough local talent, cheap contracts, hidden potential, weak fame.
- Downtown (Lv 3): more polished city recruits, broader tags, higher costs.
- Empire (Lv 4-5): premium stars, high impact, high cost, lower loyalty.

## Stat ranges
- Trailer: age 21-55, beauty 25-60, performance 20-65, popularity 5-35, loyalty 40-80, salary $40-$140.
- Downtown: age 21-45, beauty 45-85, performance 40-85, popularity 25-70, loyalty 30-70, salary $150-$600.
- Empire: age 21-42, beauty 65-99, performance 60-99, popularity 60-99, loyalty 20-60, salary $700-$2500.

## Archetype pools
- Trailer-focused: Trailer Park Veteran, Local Webcam Hopeful, Bar Stage Regular, Garage Glamour Girl, Small-Town Flirt, VHS Amateur, Divorced Bombshell, Rough-Cut Performer, Backyard Pin-Up, Late-Night Caller.
- Downtown/Empire introduce Glamour/Studio/Premium archetypes.

## Hidden potential types
- late_bloomer
- camera_loves_her
- loyal_workhorse
- niche_magnet
- cheap_star

Current lightweight effects: Late Bloomer gives extra popularity growth on successful releases; Loyal Workhorse improves loyalty outcomes on release results.

## Content safety rules
- Consenting adults only.
- No minors or underage-coded labels.
- No coercion, assault, trafficking, incest-coded, or intoxication-consent scenarios.

## UI language rules
Use: Rough Start, Local Talent, Hidden Potential, Amateur Appeal, Needs Better Gear, Reliable Worker.
Avoid insulting/degrading performer language.

## Save compatibility
New Girl fields are optional and have safe fallbacks in recruit/reveal UI.
Existing localStorage saves should continue loading without resets.
